# OMP Enhancer Workflow Catalog v47

Advisory reference. Main orchestrates freely through ANALYZE -> EXECUTE -> REVIEW.

## `writing`

- When: Prose and copy in any language (English, Chinese) or format (LaTeX, Markdown, Beamer, Word, PowerPoint/PPTX): Main drafts every wording surface itself and passes each passage through the polisher tool, while Main/task handle code, evidence, structure, layout, conversion, and validation.
- Skills: `writing-review`, `format-humanizer`, `plain-chinese-writing`, `zh-research-achievement-writing`, `zh-format-humanizer`, `zh-writing-review`, `zh-writing-polish`, `writing-markdown-helper`, `zh-writing-markdown-helper`, `format-markdown2latex`, `format-latex2markdown`, `format-template-latex`, `latex-beamer-slides`, `beamer-to-powerpoint`, `slides-storyline`, `storyline-workbench`, `docx`
- Agents: `task`
- Flow:
  1. Identify target language (zh/en) and format (plain/LaTeX/Markdown/Beamer/Word/PowerPoint/PPTX).
  2. Load matching language and format skills.
  3. For Chinese prose, the Chinese writing-baseline section in the bundled writing-helper/polisher/prompt-zh.md is read because it supplies a verified excerpt from Zhang et al., Recent Progress in Program Analysis (Journal of Software, 2019, 30(1), 80–109; DOI 10.13328/j.cnki.jos.005651) and the G1–G10 rules to carry into the drafting style pack, and the appropriate genre card from skill://plain-chinese-writing/references/style-profiles.md is then selected for content organization, readers, and format. Main drafting and the Chinese polisher use the same paper-based wording, syntax, and expository register across Chinese prose and copy, while the cards do not choose alternative default authors. Task facts stay separately marked. The reference read performs genre-material retrieval without activating the plain-chinese-writing Skill, so skills=none remains valid.
  4. The substantive-text sequence starts with Main drafting the text itself, covering logical and semantic revision, translation, titles, labels, captions, notes, and UI copy, and then passing every drafted passage through the polisher tool in its body language; before drafting a new outline or revising an existing draft, Main builds a chunk list in source order, with Chinese chunks at most 2,000 non-whitespace characters, English chunks at most 1,200 words, and slide chunks at most 6 pages, splitting by section, consecutive paragraphs, or consecutive pages and never padding a chunk to reach the limit, keeping a paragraph, a table with its scope note, a formula with its definition, and code with its needed explanation intact, and letting an indivisible unit over the limit become its own chunk with the exception recorded and no content truncated; Main drafts one chunk at a time from that chunk's source and its bounded adjacency context, calls polisher on the finished chunk, and then moves to the next chunk, never carrying the whole document or another chunk's round trips into the draft; independent chunks may be drafted in parallel when the host exposes a task agent, but the polisher call itself is always a separate language pass over one bounded chunk, and a host with less capacity batches them while still creating a new context per chunk. Each draft follows the target body language's built-in reference style carried by that matching text tool; mixed-language content is polished segment by segment with the matching language prompt; Main preserves semantic anchors and integrates each polisher output verbatim.
  5. Main reviews early feedback itself, or runs writing_quality_check or writing_logic_check, to report logic and evidence issues plus sentences that add no new fact, a necessary qualification, or a key logical relation, without rewriting prose; this is not the final review — the scan step below runs before the final review and delivery — and any substantive repair goes back through the polisher tool. Main integrates the exact polisher output, while code, evidence collection, structural reordering, layout, conversion, validation, and other non-text actions may remain with Main/task.
  6. If the polisher tool is unavailable or its model cannot be resolved, record that inability as a limitation and do not silently ship unpolished prose or draft through another role.
  7. For Word output, apply the polished content through officecli (docx skill) and verify with officecli validate and a rendered view; task may perform only structural, layout, conversion, and validation operations and must not change wording.
  8. For a new article or a new slide deck, build and confirm the story as a tree in the storyline-workbench site before any prose; the site freezes the confirmed tree and its open comments into an immutable round snapshot, Main writes from that snapshot and passes the text through polisher, and an already confirmed story can be loaded into the site for another round.
  9. For new Beamer decks, Main produces the Markdown content plan and passes its prose through polisher whose titles avoid the "XX：XX" label+colon+label pattern and read as coherent prose in deck order; confirm it with the user, let a separate task reconcile slide order and structure, then have the task convert the unchanged polished content plan into Beamer and begin layout.
  10. After either a new or existing Beamer deck reaches its final validated Beamer visual revision, branch optionally to PPTX output when requested: use the fixed external `beamer2pptx` Skill/repository at https://github.com/xdmlxdml/beamer2pptx/tree/main/beamer2pptx with the final validated PDF and corresponding `.tex`, macro, and font sources when available; a producing task performs only PPTX conversion, binding, rendering, and bounded fixes, then one independent read-only reviewer checks the current PPTX renders. The producing task may apply at most one bounded fix, rerender fresh evidence, and the same reviewer confirms only those findings once. Keep this branch advisory with no automatic loop or hard gate; preserve visible content, formulas, slide order, and Markdown/Beamer sources. A content or layout finding is resolved in place at the stage where it is found; it is not propagated back to the Markdown content plan; a prose wording change still goes through the polisher tool and is applied in place only as that verbatim output; task authors no wording.
  11. For substantial de-AI work, apply the format-humanizer/zh-format-humanizer rules one at a time before checking: Main inventories the tell families present, then runs one polisher pass per found family over bounded chunks; the family passes run strictly sequentially, and the next family starts only after every authorized chunk of the current family is complete and its semantic-anchor check verified by Main. All rewriting stays with the polisher and no pass mixes families.
  12. After every completed writing task, once Main has checked sentence-level information gain and absolute scope on its own final text and on the polisher output, call writing_ai_scan with detector=ensemble and allowNetwork=true on that text before delivery; when it reports a tell family, route one polisher repair pass per family and rerun the scan on the revised text, and after any other polisher repair — including wording changes motivated by orphan lines, clipping, overflow, spacing, or other layout findings — rerun the sentence-level information-gain and absolute-scope check on the exact final wording before the scan and the final review of the integrated text. When the ensemble scan cannot run or errors, record it as "ensemble unavailable" or "ensemble failed" and record any local fallback result separately under its own label; a local fallback never marks the ensemble checkpoint or TODO complete.
  13. For substantial work, Main reviews the integrated text itself, or runs writing_quality_check, to report logic, semantic, evidence, and clarity issues, including sentences that add no new fact, a necessary qualification, or a key logical relation, all read-only and without rewriting prose; a generic task, diff-review, or visual review never substitutes for this final review on substantial writing, and after a layout-motivated polisher repair the integrated final text still receives it once the sentence-level information-gain and absolute-scope check has run on the exact final wording; after chunked integration, run a read-only consistency check covering each chunk's fidelity and style against its own source, every adjacent pair's referent and continuation, document-wide terminology and subject use, repeated closers and page templateization, and style coverage across the opening, middle, and closing chunks; that check may be split across chunks, each problem is located to a specific chunk, and every wording repair goes through polisher again.
  14. Deliver with preservation and consistency verification.

## `research`

- When: Source-backed research, web synthesis, comparison, recommendation, fact-checking, or claim-by-claim verdict.
- Skills: `fact-checking`, `claim-extraction`, `source-evaluation`, `citation-authenticity`
- Agents: `fact-researcher-a`, `fact-researcher-b`, `fact-researcher-c`, `fact-challenger`, `fact-planner`, `scout`
- Flow:
  1. Decompose into checkable claims or research questions.
  2. Collect evidence from primary sources; corroborate with multiple sources.
  3. Cross-check evidence lanes for agreement, conflicts, and staleness.
  4. Route every user-facing research text through Main drafting and the polisher tool before synthesis: Main drafts the passage in its body language, calls polisher with the matching zh or en prompt, and inserts the returned text verbatim; mixed-language output is polished segment by segment.
  5. Main drafts the source-linked synthesis with confidence levels itself and passes it through polisher; code, evidence collection, source comparison, and structural work stay with the research roles and Main.
  6. Review verdicts for overclaiming; report limitations without drafting or rewriting prose.

## `visual`

- When: Diagrams (draw.io), UI/UX design, static visual artifacts, or rendered figure review.
- Skills: `drawio-skill`, `assetseeker`, `frontend-design`, `canvas-design`, `format-humanizer`, `zh-format-humanizer`
- Agents: `task`
- Flow:
  1. Clarify the diagram type, target language, information claims, real nodes and directed relations (including conditions and grouping meaning), output paths, physical width and the destination rectangle's aspect ratio (4:3 or 16:9 for PPT, page-width or half-page for A4/US letter), the at most 3 key messages the figure must foreground, and any names, math symbols, or terms that must not change. Before drawing, Main drafts every user-facing label, caption, narrative, and UI copy in its body language and passes each passage through polisher (mixed-language output is polished segment by segment); task may perform only structural mapping, drawing, layout, export, and evidence-based review, and maps the polished text verbatim. For diagram-only branches, use drawio-skill (drawio@365-skills) plus the one authoring reference for the chosen method. Asset prep is a default planning step: Main names the hero node or path, the lead decorative hue, and an icon for every concrete component node, then, unless the user asked for vector-only or text-only output, pre-generates one coherent house-style icon family with the native generate_image tool (style board as input), adds Iconify glyphs via assetseeker for standard technical symbols, assetseeker stock for photographic content, and bl image via bash for Chinese-text or photoreal nodes, recording provenance before drawing. For a new figure Main may also generate one composition thumbnail as a private layout reference; its text is ignored and it is never delivered.
  2. After Main obtains the polished copy, task derives a stable node/edge ID list from the source material, maps the text verbatim, then draws the diagram once with drawio-skill and exports: a preview PNG (no -e, longest side <= 2000), a cropped single-page vector PDF, and a final PNG (-e -s 2) repaired with the skill's repair_png.py. Ask the user only when the diagram meaning or physical size changes.
  3. Exactly one independent read-only review of the actual exports: Main when it has image input, otherwise one image-capable task that did not author the diagram (a text-only task model cannot read the exports and is never assigned the review). The reviewer checks node/edge semantics, edges crossing unrelated boxes, overlapping or collinear edges, arrow attribution, arrowhead size against the visible shaft (arrowheads small on a shaft of at least 3× their length), text clipping, font size at physical width, alignment, grouping margins, reading direction, gray-scale distinguishability, and the color quota (white/black base, no gray or pale fills, small saturated accent blocks from the fixed palette (red #D5171A failure/risk, teal #037870 success/verification, blue #1668B5 and amber #E08A1E decorative-only), no AI rainbow per-element or per-category color assignment); it also checks the aesthetic recipe — a readable hero hierarchy, content filling about 65–85% of the frame, palette ink at roughly 5–12% with an accent thread along the key message, one consistent icon family, badges anchored to their node, and the stroke and type tiers — and enforces the scientific-figure limits — node count within 5-8, at most 3 key messages dominating the canvas, labels terse, vivid icons instead of bare text boxes, and the destination frame ratio preserved in the exports; findings name node/edge IDs, location, and one executable local fix.
  4. The task applies at most one local structure/layout fix round for supported findings (keeping other node/edge IDs and positions), re-exports the same source set, and the same reviewer confirms against the fresh exports that recorded findings are resolved and no new regression was introduced; any text finding returns to the polisher tool instead of being edited by task or Main. This confirmation is not a second review pass or a new design round. Remaining findings are reported as limitations.
  5. Deliver the .drawio source plus the exports the task actually needs (default for paper/report: .drawio + cropped vector PDF + clean PNG); Main retains setup authorization and final acceptance only.
