# `writing` workflow reference

Optional advisory reference. Main orchestrates freely.

- When: Prose and copy in any language (English, Chinese) or format (LaTeX, Markdown, Beamer, Word, PowerPoint/PPTX): Main drafts every wording surface itself and passes each passage through the polisher tool, while Main/task handle code, evidence, structure, layout, conversion, and validation.
- Skills: `writing-review`, `format-humanizer`, `plain-chinese-writing`, `zh-research-achievement-writing`, `zh-format-humanizer`, `zh-writing-review`, `zh-writing-polish`, `writing-markdown-helper`, `zh-writing-markdown-helper`, `format-markdown2latex`, `format-latex2markdown`, `format-template-latex`, `latex-beamer-slides`, `beamer-to-powerpoint`, `slides-storyline`, `storyline-workbench`, `docx`
- Agent candidates: `task`.

Text capability note: `polisher` is a writing-helper tool, not an Agent. Main drafts the text, calls `polisher` on each bounded passage, and inserts the returned text verbatim; code, evidence, structure, layout, and validation stay with Main/task, and the polisher model may be older or smaller because it performs only language polishing.

## Required step order

These steps are the required execution order for this domain. The plugin provides no runtime gate, router, or completion condition — that means the runtime never blocks you, not that the steps are optional. Skipping a named step without a stated reason is a workflow violation; report it in the final delivery.

1. Identify target language (zh/en) and format (plain/LaTeX/Markdown/Beamer/Word/PowerPoint/PPTX).
2. Load matching language and format skills.
3. For Chinese prose, the Chinese writing-baseline section in the bundled writing-helper/polisher/prompt-zh.md is read because it supplies a verified excerpt from Zhang et al., Recent Progress in Program Analysis (Journal of Software, 2019, 30(1), 80–109; DOI 10.13328/j.cnki.jos.005651) and the G1–G10 rules to carry into the drafting style pack, and the appropriate genre card from skill://plain-chinese-writing/references/style-profiles.md is then selected for content organization, readers, and format. Main drafting and the Chinese polisher use the same paper-based wording, syntax, and expository register across Chinese prose and copy, while the cards do not choose alternative default authors. Task facts stay separately marked. The reference read performs genre-material retrieval without activating the plain-chinese-writing Skill, so skills=none remains valid.
4. The substantive-text sequence starts with Main drafting the text itself, covering logical and semantic revision, translation, titles, labels, captions, notes, and UI copy, and then passing every drafted passage through the polisher tool in its body language; before drafting a new outline or revising an existing draft, Main builds a chunk list in source order, with Chinese chunks at most 2,000 non-whitespace characters, English chunks at most 1,200 words, and slide chunks at most 6 pages, splitting by section, consecutive paragraphs, or consecutive pages and never padding a chunk to reach the limit, keeping a paragraph, a table with its scope note, a formula with its definition, and code with its needed explanation intact, and letting an indivisible unit over the limit become its own chunk with the exception recorded and no content truncated; Main drafts one chunk at a time from that chunk's source and its bounded adjacency context, calls polisher on the finished chunk, and then moves to the next chunk, never carrying the whole document or another chunk's round trips into the draft; independent chunks may be drafted in parallel when the host exposes a task agent, but the polisher call itself is always a separate language pass over one bounded chunk, and a host with less capacity batches them while still creating a new context per chunk. Each draft follows the target body language's built-in reference style carried by that matching text tool; mixed-language content is polished segment by segment with the matching language prompt; Main preserves semantic anchors and integrates each polisher output verbatim.
5. Main reviews early feedback itself, or runs writing_quality_check or writing_logic_check, to report logic and evidence issues plus sentences that add no new fact, a necessary qualification, or a key logical relation, without rewriting prose; this is not the final review — the scan step below runs before the final review and delivery — and any substantive repair goes back through the polisher tool. Main integrates the exact polisher output, while code, evidence collection, structural reordering, layout, conversion, validation, and other non-text actions may remain with Main/task.
6. If the polisher tool is unavailable or its model cannot be resolved, record that inability as a limitation and do not silently ship unpolished prose or draft through another role.
7. For Word output, apply the polished content through officecli (docx skill) and verify with officecli validate and a rendered view; task may perform only structural, layout, conversion, and validation operations and must not change wording.
8. For a new article or a new slide deck, build and confirm the story as a tree in the storyline-workbench site before any prose; the site freezes the confirmed tree and its open comments into an immutable round snapshot, Main writes from that snapshot and passes the text through polisher, and an already confirmed story can be loaded into the site for another round.
9. For new Beamer decks, Main produces the Markdown content plan and passes its prose through polisher whose titles avoid the "XX：XX" label+colon+label pattern and read as coherent prose in deck order; confirm it with the user, let a separate task reconcile slide order and structure, then have the task convert the unchanged polished content plan into Beamer and begin layout.
10. After either a new or existing Beamer deck reaches its final validated Beamer visual revision, branch optionally to PPTX output when requested: use the fixed external `beamer2pptx` Skill/repository at https://github.com/xdmlxdml/beamer2pptx/tree/main/beamer2pptx with the final validated PDF and corresponding `.tex`, macro, and font sources when available; a producing task performs only PPTX conversion, binding, rendering, and bounded fixes, then one independent read-only reviewer checks the current PPTX renders. The producing task may apply at most one bounded fix, rerender fresh evidence, and the same reviewer confirms only those findings once. Keep this branch advisory with no automatic loop or hard gate; preserve visible content, formulas, slide order, and Markdown/Beamer sources. A content or layout finding is resolved in place at the stage where it is found; it is not propagated back to the Markdown content plan; a prose wording change still goes through the polisher tool and is applied in place only as that verbatim output; task authors no wording.
11. For substantial de-AI work, apply the format-humanizer/zh-format-humanizer rules one at a time before checking: Main inventories the tell families present, then runs one polisher pass per found family over bounded chunks; the family passes run strictly sequentially, and the next family starts only after every authorized chunk of the current family is complete and its semantic-anchor check verified by Main. All rewriting stays with the polisher and no pass mixes families.
12. After every completed writing task, once Main has checked sentence-level information gain and absolute scope on its own final text and on the polisher output, call writing_ai_scan with detector=ensemble and allowNetwork=true on that text before delivery; when it reports a tell family, route one polisher repair pass per family and rerun the scan on the revised text, and after any other polisher repair — including wording changes motivated by orphan lines, clipping, overflow, spacing, or other layout findings — rerun the sentence-level information-gain and absolute-scope check on the exact final wording before the scan and the final review of the integrated text. When the ensemble scan cannot run or errors, record it as "ensemble unavailable" or "ensemble failed" and record any local fallback result separately under its own label; a local fallback never marks the ensemble checkpoint or TODO complete.
13. For substantial work, Main reviews the integrated text itself, or runs writing_quality_check, to report logic, semantic, evidence, and clarity issues, including sentences that add no new fact, a necessary qualification, or a key logical relation, all read-only and without rewriting prose; a generic task, diff-review, or visual review never substitutes for this final review on substantial writing, and after a layout-motivated polisher repair the integrated final text still receives it once the sentence-level information-gain and absolute-scope check has run on the exact final wording; after chunked integration, run a read-only consistency check covering each chunk's fidelity and style against its own source, every adjacent pair's referent and continuation, document-wide terminology and subject use, repeated closers and page templateization, and style coverage across the opening, middle, and closing chunks; that check may be split across chunks, each problem is located to a specific chunk, and every wording repair goes through polisher again.
14. Deliver with preservation and consistency verification.

## Scope notes

- Language selection: detect the target body language of the deliverable rather than the instruction language; Main drafts in that language and calls polisher with the matching zh or en prompt, and mixed-language content is polished segment by segment.
- The language-matched text tool carries a built-in base style for its language; a user-specified genre, length, terminology, template, and factual anchors win over that base style, and the base style never bends, invents, or drops evidence to fit itself.
- The default Chinese style for future prose and copy is anchored in Zhang et al., Recent Progress in Program Analysis, Journal of Software, 2019, 30(1), 80–109, DOI 10.13328/j.cnki.jos.005651. The Chinese polisher prompt bundles two verified excerpts totalling 82 characters from section 2.3, with separate page references, G1–G10 rules, and compact contrastive examples. Main reads the same anchor and rules in skill://plain-chinese-writing/references/style-profiles.md before drafting, and carries only the applicable extended example into the current chunk's style pack. The extended examples cover concept definitions, method mechanisms, and scope and comparison; they are independently authored by the plugin, not quotations, translations, or expanded reproductions of the paper, and they stay on the drafting side and are not appended to each polisher call. This replaces the earlier personal-research sample as the default style. The style uses accurate terminology and an objective written register, and defines the object before explaining its mechanism, classification, applicable conditions, and limits where needed, while it keeps necessary compound sentences, explicit assumptions, sourced uncertainty, and consistent comparison criteria. It does not impose a fixed paragraph shape or require a 针对 opener, a 结果显示 lead, or a contribution closer in each paragraph. The six genre cards determine organization, readers, and format rather than selecting another style reference. Titles, labels, captions, interface copy, slide bullets, and notices retain their own length and format, with the addressee and requested action preserved in notices. User-specified style for the current task overrides the default, while local Skill wording preferences yield to the baseline. Facts, terminology, numbers, citations, scope, modality, and hard phrasing bans never yield to style. The excerpts and examples supply style only; do not transfer their subject matter, people, tools, measurements, citations, examples, or claims to unrelated tasks or repeat them in deliverables. These materials are bundled, so future writing does not require network retrieval; when the baseline file is missing, report that limitation and follow the stated rules without inventing an excerpt. Evidence-backed contribution closers and quantified results stay scoped to the 科研成果凝练 and 学术正文 genres, and never become a paragraph template or permission to invent a result.
- LaTeX/Beamer/Word/Markdown are format overlays, not separate workflows; select matching format skills.
- Short copy and format surfaces (titles, labels, captions, interface copy, slide bullets) still compress within their own format constraints and are not required to carry all three of problem, method, and result, but they never drop a result or its qualifiers for the sake of brevity when they do state one; the grammatical subject follows what the task or the material specifies, a specific person, a team or project name, or a system name, and a user-specified subject is never overridden; only when neither the user nor the material specifies one does the subject fall back to 本人 for personal materials, 本文 or 我们 for papers and team work, or the work carrying the result; when revising existing prose that the user has not asked to re-style, keep the original wording and register instead of rewriting it wholesale into the narrative register; a style, terminology, or length the user specifies for the current task takes precedence over the baseline, and factual anchors and hard phrasing bans hold unchanged.
- Each Chinese drafting chunk carries the paper-based baseline and bundled excerpt from writing-helper/polisher/prompt-zh.md, the matching genre card, the target readers and subject identity, the terminology list and phrasing bans, and separately marked task facts and citations, where the paper governs wording and exposition and the card governs organization and format. Selection of a different default sample by genre and inheritance of the earlier personal-research paragraph template are excluded, whereas research-achievement structure is used only when that is the requested genre and contribution closers with a named object and scope and quantified effect statements carrying a measured number and a baseline remain scoped to the 科研成果凝练 and 学术正文 cards. Necessary conditions and sourced uncertainty stay intact across genres.
- Word (.docx) documents are produced and edited through officecli (single binary, no Office install): Main drafts and polishes the content, applies it with officecli add/set, then verifies with officecli validate and view issues, and renders view html/screenshot for visual confirmation before delivery. officecli also covers .xlsx and .pptx, and its merge command fills {{key}} templates from JSON.
- Every English or Chinese prose/copy surface is drafted by Main and then rewritten by the polisher tool before it enters the deliverable: drafting, revision, translation, titles, labels, captions, notes, narrative copy, and UI copy all finish with a polisher call; Main preserves semantic anchors and integrates each returned text verbatim; writing_quality_check reports logic and evidence issues without rewriting prose, and any substantive repair goes back through polisher. Main/task own code, evidence collection, structural reordering, layout, conversion, validation, and other non-text actions.
- Information-gain principle, always on for every prose and copy surface in every language, including when no Skill is loaded: each sentence serves one clear expressive focus and keeps only what adds a new fact, a necessary qualification, or a key logical relation; do not re-explain what the reader can infer from the surrounding text, do not use modifiers with no information gain, do not narrate a process step by step only for completeness, and allow a reasonable omission so the reader completes the simple inference — when revising, delete the redundant wording before writing more words to explain the same point. Deletion removes redundant expression, never unique evidence: a focused sentence may carry several necessary clauses, so the rule never forces one fact per sentence or uniformly short sentences, and unique facts, numbers, units, qualifiers, scope, modality, negation, causal direction, citations, safety instructions, reproducibility steps, and task-required content all stay. When Main extracts semantic and factual anchors for a drafting pass, abstract restatements and concessive empty closers are not independent facts to preserve, so the underlying fact is kept while the empty close is deleted, even if the source text or an earlier assignment explicitly lists the closer itself as required content. Main applies the principle while drafting and revising, so the sentence-level check runs before the ensemble scan; writing_quality_check reports violations read-only in the clarity review when that review is used; neither the AIGC+Jev authorship score nor the local scanner metrics can replace this sentence-level review, which stays advisory method guidance rather than a runtime gate or an authorship verdict.
- If the language-matched text tool cannot safely handle substantive prose, record the inability as a limitation and do not silently draft through another role.
- For new Beamer decks, build and confirm the deck story in the storyline-workbench site, then have Main write the per-page Markdown content plan from that confirmed snapshot and pass every page's text through polisher; the content plan decides each page's form, and a table is proposed only when it serves comparison or lookup on that page, so the same page does not repeat the table row by row in prose; after the user confirms each page's content, a separate task reconciles the slide order on the unchanged plan (no content overlap, semantically coherent modules, logical overall progression; page titles avoid the "XX：XX" label+colon+label pattern and read as coherent prose in deck order; reconfirm only on material reorders; unfixable crossings return to Markdown reconfirmation) before task converts the plan into Beamer and begins visual authoring and basic layout. Plan→Beamer is forward, one-way, and explicitly requested; the Markdown plan is the source for that explicit generation and Beamer .tex files are derived layout artifacts of that pass. Three independent editing modes — storyline-only (plan), Beamer-only (.tex/PDF), and PPTX-only (.pptx) — keep ordinary edits inside the chosen target; the target's current revision is the edit baseline, counterparts are read-only context, divergence does not trigger realignment, and an isolated edit never propagates silently forward or backward.
- The storyline-workbench site is the story-organization step for a new article or a new slide deck; an already confirmed tree stays editable there for later rounds, and local polishing, translation, or edits to an existing finished document keep that document's own revision as the baseline and do not start a new tree.
- Each leaf is one article paragraph or one deck page titled with its topic sentence; the root holds the whole topic, and chapters and sections group the leaves. A slide plan writes one page per leaf in tree order and records that leaf ID in `sourceNodeIds` together with `storyRevision`, while an article turns chapters and sections into headings and each leaf into one paragraph from the exported snapshot.
- Direct PPTX editing is distinct from PPTX as a Beamer output: editing an existing .pptx is the PPTX-only mode and goes through the officecli-backed docx flow; an isolated PPTX edit does not regenerate from Beamer. The optional Beamer→PPTX path (fixed external beamer2pptx Skill/repository) consumes the final validated Beamer PDF as the visual authority and does not consult or update the Markdown plan.
- Chinese slide copy follows the 幻灯片正文 card (one job per page, at most three bullets, complete short sentences, no contribution closer, and no forced 针对 opener or 结果显示 lead) and uses plain-chinese-writing for natural sentences, zh-format-humanizer for AI-like phrasing, and zh-writing-review for page-level clarity; the research-narrative register does not apply to slide copy; English slide copy uses format-humanizer for AI-tell removal and writing-review for page-level review; use zh-writing-polish only for actual polishing and never replace body prose with keyword or phrase lists.
- Every writing task ends with a writing_ai_scan check with detector=ensemble and allowNetwork=true on the final text before delivery; the scan is advisory evidence rather than a gate, findings return to the polisher tool, and the last scan result is recorded with the delivery. When AIGC_DETECTOR_URL is missing, no Jev source is available (neither TYPESAFE_API_KEY nor an OMP judge model), or the tool is unavailable, record that limitation instead of substituting a keyword-only or local-only judgement. When the ensemble scan itself cannot run or errors, record it as "ensemble unavailable" or "ensemble failed"; any local fallback runs only as a separately labelled record and never marks the ensemble checkpoint or TODO complete, and nothing in this note adds a runtime gate or an automatic retry loop.
- De-AI is one-sighting in every language: any listed tell justifies an edit on its first appearance — prefer an over-correction to a miss ("宁可误杀，不可放过"). Only quotations, titles, proper names, passages discussing a phrase rather than using it, text predating November 30, 2022, and details that carry the author's voice (specific unusual details, mixed feelings, first-person choices) are exempt.
- De-AI application is per-rule, not one mixed pass: for substantial documents, Main first inventories which tell families actually appear (contrast-repetition, one-line closers, sayings that sound deep, staged run-up, arguing with no one, forced triads, repeated openings, dashes, stacked qualifiers, hyphenated pairs, passive/missing subjects, symmetrical paragraphs, inflated words, inflated significance, -ing riders, sales language, borrowed authority, vague connection, copula avoidance, bold decoration, decorative headings, curly quotes, chatbot residue, knowledge-limit disclaimers, repeated headings, writing about previous versions, anaphoric passive riders, anaphoric callback clauses; zh: 否定式对偶、单句收尾、貌似深刻的说法、铺垫式开场、与不存在的对手辩论、意义拔高、理解承诺句、抽象复述回声、宣布式过渡、节奏对仗收尾、回指被动尾句、回指回扣小句、及弱特征清单), then runs one polisher pass per found tell family over bounded chunks. The next family starts only after every authorized chunk of the current family is complete and its semantic-anchor check is verified (限定/模态/范围/否定/方向/数字/引用 unchanged; no fact added or dropped). A family's round becomes the new accepted version only when all its authorized chunks are done, so no later family or the user sees half old and half new text. Each polisher pass handles only its one family on that chunk's bounded text. Skip families the scan did not find.
- Hard phrasing bans in every deliverable (prose, slides, figures, documents): no "不是X，而是Y" contrast-repetition construction or its English equivalents ("not X, but Y", "not just X") — state the claim once, directly, with no restatement in inverted or synonym form; no opening or closing one-sentence summary that restates what the text just showed; and, in structured artifacts, no "XX：XX" label+colon+label titles or label-colon bullet lead-ins, and no claim or label spliced onto a colon in titles, bullets, or captions (a colon is allowed only before an enumeration of three or more items, a definition, a formula, or code, and split captions "Left: …; Right: …" are rewritten as complete sentences). These are fixed whenever found, not clustering-gated tells. Verbatim quotations and cited material are exempt.
- Hard phrasing bans in every deliverable also cover the setup then emphasis then restatement pattern, where one sentence sets up, a second emphasizes the same claim, and a third restates the same idea in other words, so the sentence carrying the fact is kept and the restatement is deleted; and unscoped absolutes, where all, any, only, always, never, every, must, exactly, and their Chinese equivalents 都, 只能, 任何, 一定, 必然, 始终, 绝不, 全部, 唯一 are kept only when the stated scope is present and matches the evidence and otherwise narrowed or deleted with the scope, the exception, or the untested case stated. These two patterns are fixed whenever found, not clustering-gated tells. Verbatim quotations and cited material are exempt, and qualifiers and preconditions are not collateral: necessary conditions, scope, data provenance, measurement conditions, and untested cases are never deleted along with the offending restatement, and these bans are never a length cap, never a gate, and never a reason to drop a semantic anchor.
- Hard phrasing bans also cover anaphoric passive riders, 回指被动尾句: 完整主句后用逗号接「前置宾语＋由＋回指施事＋动词」的小句，把同一次操作里的另一个动作另起一截（「Documents 和 report.txt 拼成一条路径，斜杠由它补上。」）。把尾句的动作和新对象并进主句，用「并」接在主句动词后面；施事能从前文还原就写一次名称，还原不出来保留回指或拆成独立句，不臆造名称。纯重复归 S13，本族只处理带动作或新对象的尾句。 English §38 reads: **§38 Anaphoric passive riders.** A complete main clause followed by a comma and a `with` phrase in the shape with + object + past participle + by + an anaphoric actor; the `with` phrase is a participial adjunct, never a finite clause, and the rider carries the operation's second action behind an anaphoric actor. Before: "Documents and report.txt are joined into one path, with the separator added by it." After: "The function joins Documents and report.txt and adds the separator." The fix merges the rider's action and new object into the main clause; no deletion branch exists. Write the actor's name once only when it is recoverable from the main clause or earlier context; when it is not recoverable, keep the anaphor or split the rider into an independent sentence, and never invent an actor. §36 covers pure repetition, §18 covers shallow -ing riders, and §38 covers anaphoric passive riders carrying an action or a new object. Exemptions: independent sentences, explicit non-anaphoric actors, quotations, proper names, and code; no blanket exemption for interface documentation or technical passive voice. This is fixed whenever found, not clustering-gated.
- Hard phrasing bans also cover anaphoric callback clauses, 回指回扣小句: 完整主句后用逗号接以「这」「那」「此」开头的回指小句，小句只给前面的整句作一次判断、使役或定性（「浏览器把这三样凑齐，这就是万维网。」）。尾句带主句缺的事实、条件或对象就并入主句，只是贴标签就删掉；名称、数字、日期和限定照原文取用。 English §39 reads: **Anaphoric callback clauses.** A complete main clause followed by a comma and then a clause headed by this, that, which, or these whose predicate only labels or stamps what precedes — a copula, a causative, or an abstract category such as "the product of" or "the starting point of". Before: "The browser assembles the three pieces, which is the World Wide Web." After: "The browser assembles HTML, URL, and HTTP into the World Wide Web." Fold a tail that carries a fact into the main clause; delete a tail that only names or stamps what the main clause already said; never invent a fact or a name to complete the merged sentence. §36 covers pure repetition, §18 covers shallow -ing riders, §38 covers anaphoric passive riders carrying an action or a new object, and this rule covers anaphoric callback clauses. Independent sentences, quotations, proper names, code, and tails carrying a measured number or a new condition are exempt, with no blanket exemption for technical documentation. The self-check runs while drafting as well as while revising.
- No "specifics then sweep" summary clause: do not follow a concrete factual clause with a summarizing clause that elevates it to a sweeping whole ("……。这些共同构成了……/这一切标志着……/正是这些……成就了……"; English "Together, these ...", "All of this marked ...", "It was these ... that ..."). The tell has two faces: the second clause adds no new fact, only inflation; and its skeleton mirrors the first clause (same object, scope adverb, agent, completion verb), forming a loose pseudo-parallel couplet. This is the documentary-narration "画面＋全称升华" two-beat sentence — a stock template of AI-written popular history. Fix by keeping the concrete clause and cutting the sweep, or by replacing the sweep with a real fact.
- No "understanding promise" transitions ("理解了 X，Y 就好懂了", "把这个说清楚，A、B 和 C 就容易理解了", "X is the key to understanding Y"): an empty promise about the reader's future comprehension with no stated dependency — delete it, or state the real dependency concretely ("A、B、C 都以 X 为基础"), or, when the text really covers the listed items in order, write the roadmap instead ("先讲 X，再看 A、B 和 C"). This is fixed whenever found, not clustering-gated.
- No abstract-restatement echo ("算筹、算盘就能做加减乘除。" followed by "“会算”这件事人类很早就做到了。"): the second clause repeats the first at a higher abstraction level with zero information gain. This is the sibling of the "specifics then sweep" ban without the sweeping whole — the test is the same two-beat skeleton (concrete fact, then a quoted-or-nominalized abstraction of that same fact). Fix by keeping the concrete clause and deleting the echo, or replacing it with a fact that actually advances the text. Never wrap a common-sense statement in quotation marks or nominalize it into a concept object ("“会算”这件事") — the text does not put scare quotes around its own commonplaces. This is fixed whenever found, not clustering-gated.
- No "the real question is" announcer transitions ("新的问题是……", "真正的问题是……", "这就引出了一个更深的问题……", "The real question is ..."): the text does not declare on the reader's behalf which question matters next; the facts themselves carry the transition, or a concrete dependency states it ("在此基础上，还剩两个障碍"). Announcing significance is narration-voice, natural when spoken in a lecture and filler once written down. This is fixed whenever found, not clustering-gated.
- For Beamer/PPT titles, body, captions, labels, notes, and narrative copy, no announcer transitions used only as stage announcements (including "The real question is", "A new question is", "This raises a deeper question", "Let us turn to", "新的问题是", "真正的问题是", "这就引出了一个更深的问题", and "接下来我们看"); let facts or a concrete dependency carry the transition instead.
- For Beamer/PPT titles, body, captions, labels, notes, and narrative copy, no unsupported hollow significance/evaluation assertions (including "this is important/significant/transformative", "this demonstrates the power/value", "意义重大", "具有重要意义", "标志着", "彰显了", "开创了", and "充分说明"); state concrete evidence, scope, or source instead, and retain significance/evaluation only when that support is stated.
- No rhythm-matched paired-phrase closer: do not end a sentence, paragraph, or slide with two or more neatly matched phrases ("够不够快、能不能自动", "更便宜、更安全、更可控", "faster, cheaper, better") unless each named item is a real, independently justified dimension of the claim. Matched rhythm is earned by content, not composed for cadence. If one of the parallel items would be cut without losing meaning, cut the whole flourish and state the single point plainly. This is fixed whenever found, not clustering-gated.
- No didactic cognition directives in any deliverable: cognition verbs with resultative complements aimed at the reader or the text itself — zh: 分清楚、分清、看清楚、看清、看明白、认清、想清楚、想明白、搞清楚、弄清楚、理清楚、理顺、捋顺、记住、请注意、要注意、别搞混、不要混淆、吃透、掰开揉碎、把握好、划重点、这就说清楚了、至此脉络理清了、想必你已经明白，以及绝对化补语 定死、框死、锁死、堵死、卡死、限死; en: "distinguish clearly", "see clearly", "keep in mind", "remember that", "note this carefully", "don't confuse X with Y", "this makes it clear", "now the picture is complete", "nailed down", "locked in". State the distinction, the conclusion, or the analysis itself; when a command precedes real analysis, delete the command and keep the analysis; when expressing a fixed choice, give the concrete value or constraint and say which parts remain adjustable. Exemptions: legitimate instructional registers (step-by-step manuals, dialogue, interactive teaching), fixed technical terms ("写死", "lock-free"), quotations, and proper names. This is fixed whenever found, not clustering-gated.
- No defensive writing in any deliverable: never pad a claim with hedging qualifiers that dilute it — 可能/或许/大概/某种程度上/在一定条件下/通常来说, "可能/possibly/perhaps/somewhat/relatively/fairly/quite", "it is worth noting that", "值得注意的是", "generally speaking", "to some extent" — unless the hedge is itself a sourced fact (measured variance, a cited confidence interval, a genuinely known scope limit). Never add self-protective filler: boilerplate disclaimers, "本文仅代表个人观点", over-attribution ("许多研究表明" without a citation), apologia for limitations nobody asked about, or a paragraph that argues against its own claim before making it. State the result and its actual scope; cut the armor.
- No claim-colon skeleton: a colon splices a label or short claim onto a sentence in a title, bullet, or caption ("Latency: half", "方法：分两阶段", split captions "Left: …; Right: …") and leaves the subject, verb, or the relation between the halves unstated. A colon is allowed only before an enumeration of three or more items, a definition, a formula, or code; split captions become complete sentences, and table headers are exempt. Fix by writing the sentence directly. This is fixed whenever found, not clustering-gated.
- No fixed page shape: when nearly every frame carries the same bullet count, the template set the shape. The per-page bullet count follows that page's content, never a fixed three, and no page is padded to three. This is fixed whenever found, not clustering-gated.
- No cross-slide refrain: one claim or a phrase of six or more characters repeated verbatim across three or more frames. Write a definition in full once and allow it on a summary or index page at most once more; replace the remaining occurrences with a new fact or delete them. This is fixed whenever found, not clustering-gated.
- No caption echo: a caption that restates its frame's title or its bullets (two-character shingle overlap at or above half), or a bullet that restates the title. Delete the echo or state what the figure or the page adds beyond the title and the bullets. This is fixed whenever found, not clustering-gated.

When prose and the table duplicate each other, keep the form that serves this page and carry every unique fact, qualifier, number, citation, and safety note from the dropped form into the kept one; never default to protecting the table and deleting the prose. Do not add conclusions to fill cells, keep a table the user explicitly requests, and set no table-deletion quota. Same-page duplication is the defect itself, so prose that already covers the table's rows never justifies keeping the table and the table's shape never justifies dropping it. Information unique to the dropped form, such as approximation and conditional qualifiers, definitions, and judgments, moves into the kept form before the dropped one goes. When the user limits the change to the table, prose outside it stays untouched, and a sentence the material already carries is never deleted on the grounds that Main sees no support for it; any real doubt about such a sentence is listed separately as an open verification item, while the sentence itself stays. This is fixed whenever found, not clustering-gated.

- Sentence-level information gain and emphasis, one-sighting in every language: after writing a sentence, check whether its second half adds a new fact, a necessary qualifier, or a key logical relation, and delete a second half that only re-emphasizes or restates the first half so the sentence ends where its information ends, while a second half carrying a necessary qualifier or fact is kept; cut consecutive emphasis, with no stacked assertions or intensifiers across a sentence or adjacent sentences that restate the same claim; replace repeated explanation with concrete information, explaining a concept fully once and then carrying it forward with the term plus new facts, conditions, scope, method, or cost not yet stated, supplying new information instead of another restatement when more explanation is needed; avoid the setup then emphasis then restatement pattern, where one sentence sets up, a second emphasizes the same claim, and a third restates the same idea in other words, so the sentence carrying the fact is kept and the restatement is deleted, with new information added only when more explanation is genuinely needed; and keep necessary preconditions written out, including applicable conditions, scope, data provenance, measurement conditions, and untested cases. Absolute-quantifier scope check: all, any, only, always, never, every, must, exactly, and their Chinese equivalents 都, 只能, 任何, 一定, 必然, 始终, 绝不, 全部, 唯一 are kept only when the stated scope is present and matches the evidence, and otherwise narrowed or deleted with the scope, the exception, or the untested case stated, so preconditions stay clear and the sentence never reads as unjustified certainty. This is a one-sighting style rule, never a length cap, never a gate, and never a reason to delete a qualifier, a precondition, or a semantic anchor.
